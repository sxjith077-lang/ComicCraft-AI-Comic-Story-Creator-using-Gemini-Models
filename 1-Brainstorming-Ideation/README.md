Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

Problem Identified:

Creating engaging comic stories and illustrations can be difficult and time-consuming, especially for users who do not have advanced writing or artistic skills.

Idea:

Build an AI-powered comic story creator using Gemini Models that can generate creative stories, characters, dialogues, and comic scenes based on the user's ideas or prompts.

Why This Idea:

- Combines story generation, character creation, and comic scene generation in one app.
- Uses generative AI to create creative and personalized comic content.
- Makes comic creation simple and accessible to everyone.
- Helps users turn their ideas into complete comic stories quickly.
- Simple enough to build and demonstrate within the project timeline.

Project Name Chosen:

ComicCraft – AI Comic Story Creator using Gemini Models
